FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is Ledger a real bank?＋

No. It is a portfolio-grade full-stack banking demonstration. It does not connect to real banking or payment rails.

Are the transactions real?＋

Transactions are processed inside the local demo backend and SQLite database only.

What makes this full-stack?＋

The project includes authentication, protected APIs, account data, transaction logic and persistence rather than a frontend-only mockup.
